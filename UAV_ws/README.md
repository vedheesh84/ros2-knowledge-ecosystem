# UAV_ws

**Aerial Robotics & Swarm Coordination Workspace**

This workspace serves as the primary development, simulation, and experimentation environment for aerial drones and multi-agent swarms within the Intelligent Systems Knowledge Ecosystem.

---

## 1. Overview & Architecture

`UAV_ws` hosts the micro-quadcopter flight controllers, formation flight algorithms, and distributed multi-agent swarm platforms.

For the full learning kit, progressive demos, and failure breakers, refer to:
👉 **[ros2_drone_swarm_kit](../Ros2%20learning%20kits/ros2_drone_swarm_kit/README.md)**

---

## 2. Core Capabilities

- **Flight Dynamics & Control:** 3D waypoint navigation, position PID control, and altitude hold.
- **Multi-Robot Orchestration:** Dynamic namespacing (`/drone_0`, `/drone_1`, `/drone_2`, ...).
- **Formation Engines:** Leader-follower tracking and online formation geometry switching (V-Shape, Line, Circle).
- **Swarm Coordination:** Decentralized artificial potential fields and cooperative grid coverage.
